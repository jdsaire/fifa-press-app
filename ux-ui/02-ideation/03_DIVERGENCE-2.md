## 2. Second-degree reading of the three most unrealistic

The point is not to discard these. It is to find what each was reaching for and ask whether that outcome can be delivered by another mechanism, without flattening the innovation out of it on the way.

### ID-20 — the pre-cleared press entry channel

**What it was actually trying to achieve.** Not a visa. It was trying to make accreditation *mean something* — to close the gap where a journalist is formally approved by the organiser and still stopped at a border, holding a credential that says yes from an organisation whose yes does not count where it matters.

**The emotional and cognitive outcome it reached for.** Coherence. One status, one answer, one place to look — rather than two authorities issuing contradictory verdicts on the same trip, neither of which acknowledges the other exists.

**How the outcome survives without the mechanism.** FIFA cannot pre-clear entry, but the platform can stop pretending the second authority is not there. Model entry status as a visible, named, non-FIFA-owned field alongside accreditation status — with the deciding authority stated, the incompatibility flagged when the class becomes known, and the honest line that FIFA is not the decision-maker. Coherence delivered as *one place that shows both answers and says who owns each*, rather than as one answer. **Preserved as ID-11 extended; the pre-clearance mechanism is dead, the single-surface idea is not.**

### ID-21 — the floating reserve reallocated directly to eliminated teams' press

**What it was actually trying to achieve.** It was refusing the premise. The HMW asks how to tell her better; this asks why she should be told anything, since the thing being communicated is a loss she did not cause and cannot appeal. It reaches past communication to the underlying entitlement.

**The emotional and cognitive outcome it reached for.** Continuity — that the coverage job she was sent to do survives her team's elimination, because J3 (keep filing after the team goes out) is a real job and the current system quietly makes it impossible.

**How the outcome survives without the mechanism.** FIFA cannot hand out places over the MA's head; that is the quota-politics boundary. But continuity does not require a reserve — it requires that the moment of elimination be treated as a transition with a *forward* path rather than only a contraction. What remains available to her, what her outlet's remaining entitlement is, what reopens after the group stage: making that visible at the moment the quota moves converts a dead end into a decision. **The reserve is dead. "A change carries what is still possible, not only what is gone" survives, and lands on Principle 2 — a refusal is a message, not a wall.**

### ID-23 — the self-updating physical credential

**What it was actually trying to achieve.** It was solving for the barrier specifically: the moment where her phone has no signal, the gate has a printed list, and the only reliable object in the interaction is the card in her hand. The card is trusted and current; the phone is current and untrusted; nothing is both.

**The emotional and cognitive outcome it reached for.** Standing at the barrier without exposure — G6. Not having to argue, not having to call an editor, not spending twenty minutes at kickoff minus twenty proving something.

**How the outcome survives without the mechanism.** Bespoke e-ink hardware across six countries in 2030 is a procurement programme, not a design decision. But the underlying insight is that *the object present at the point of enforcement must carry the current state*, and there are cheaper carriers: a device-held state that survives offline and is legibly timestamped (ID-10), and a gate that reads from the same event stream rather than from a morning print-out (ID-03). **The hardware is dead. "State must be present at the barrier, not merely retrievable" survives and is the reason ID-03 and ID-10 are both R3/R2 rather than optional.**

**ID-24 and ID-30 survive with a stated hazard.** Neither crosses a boundary row, but both publish an individual's access state into a space she does not control — a colleague's group, or a public feed. Gate 4 must weigh that; a concept that fixes the grapevine by turning her status into gossip has solved the wrong half of P-e.
