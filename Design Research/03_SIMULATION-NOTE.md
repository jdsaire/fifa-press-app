**Generation logic.** Both files were authored directly against the Gate 1 sample design and Gate 2 instrument, not sampled from any model of a real population. Survey rows were built archetype-by-archetype to hit the exact quota in `01_RESEARCH-PLAN.md` §4 (mid-size national outlet 8, freelance 6, broadcaster 4, wire/photo 4, NRH 3, host-city 3, digital creator 2), then confederation labels assigned within each archetype to approximate the coarse 9/5/5/5/5/1 split. Interview participants were written one-to-one against the five planned IDs and their assigned failure moment from §4.

**Archetype distribution.** Response tendencies per archetype were anchored to that archetype's documented Run 1 pains (D4, D5) so a wire agency respondent leans toward Q1/Q3 confusion (matchday-position pain) and a freelance respondent leans toward Q6/Q7 (weak appeal path), rather than every archetype answering identically. Archetype quota was hit exactly (8/6/4/4/3/3/2, matching `01_RESEARCH-PLAN.md` §4). The secondary confederation split landed at UEFA 10 · CONMEBOL 7 · CAF 2 · AFC 5 · CONCACAF 6 · OFC 0, against a stated target of 9/5/5/5/5/1 — close but not exact, and CAF under-represented relative to plan. Logged here rather than silently corrected, since the plan called this stratification "coarse" and did not set a tolerance.

**Variance and disconfirming-response injection.** Roughly one in five survey rows and one of five interview participants was deliberately written against the hypotheses: at least 4 of 30 survey rows pick option (a) on Q6 (speed over reason, disconfirming H1); at least 4 of 30 answer Q1 correctly despite low self-reported guidance-reading, or incorrectly despite high reading (disconfirming H2); one interview participant (P04) is written as broadly satisfied with the current system and skeptical that more messaging would help. Non-response and "not sure" answers appear on Q1, Q8 and Q9 rather than every field being cleanly filled, since real survey data is never complete.

**Limitations.** This file describes how plausible-sounding data was constructed from Run 1's documented pain points, not how real respondents actually think. The distribution of opinions, the rate of disconfirmation, and the correlation (or lack of one) between experience and comprehension were all authorial choices, not measurements. Any pattern found in Gate 4 reflects the assumptions encoded here before analysis began, and cannot be read as evidence about actual journalists.
